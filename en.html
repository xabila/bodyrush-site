How much space do I need in front of my iPhone or iPad?

Place your iPhone or iPad about 6 to 8 feet (1.8m – 2.5m) in front of you on a coffee table, TV stand or tripod. The smart framing guide turns green as soon as your full body is visible.

Can I play without jumping (in an apartment) or without squats?

Yes! Every level explicitly displays its movement rules: Levels 01 & 05 are 100% No-Jump & No-Squat (Boxing & Dodging), Levels 02 & 07 are No-Jump (Postures & Squats), and Levels 03 & 06 are No-Squat (Parkour & Jumps). Plus, FIT Levels 05 to 09 feature Unlimited Hearts (♾️) for uninterrupted 5 or 10-minute cardio workouts!

How does the Neon Blades style change the number of obstacles?

Moving from EASY (~40 events) to NORMAL (~80 + x2 Combos), HARD (~125 + x2/x3 Combos) or EXPERT+ (~175 + x3 Combo flurries) multiplies the density of obstacles and chained actions synced to musical accents!

How does BodyRush 3D compare to Nex Playground for family TV motion gaming?

Nex Playground requires buying a dedicated $249 camera cube plus an $89/year Play Pass. BodyRush 3D runs directly on the iPhone or iPad you already own, streams wirelessly in 16:9 at 60 FPS to your TV via AirPlay, tracks 19 skeletal joints on-device with Apple Vision, and includes both kid-friendly worlds (Toy Kingdom, Santa Village) and high-intensity 142 BPM rhythm boxing & 2–4 player Pass & Play tournaments.

Is BodyRush 3D a good alternative to Supernatural VR, Beat Saber, or Peloton Guide without wearing a headset?

Yes. Supernatural and Beat Saber on Meta Quest deliver awesome beat-synced workouts, but strapping a 500g headset to your face causes sweaty foam, fogged lenses, and isolates you from family. BodyRush 3D brings 4 Neon Blades musical difficulty tiers (up to ~175 events and x3 bass-drop combo flurries in Expert+) to your open living-room TV with zero headset and full leg + arm tracking.
